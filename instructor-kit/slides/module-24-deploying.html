10.2 · Caching

Forever for some files, never for one

  • /assets/*: max-age=31536000, immutable
  • index.html: checked on every visit (the host's default)
  • /*: nosniff, Referrer-Policy, X-Frame-Options DENY
  • Never a year-long cache on /*: your next deploy would be invisible
