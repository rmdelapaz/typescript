# Session 26 labs: Final Project Showcase and Final Assessment

Session 26 teaches no new lesson: it is the final project showcase and the written final assessment. Its one lab
is a tool for your final project.

| Folder | What it is | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| `showcase-preflight/` | `evidence_check.mjs`: run it on your final project two days before the showcase. It reads your project's files and lists the evidence Lesson 11.1's rubric asks for that is still missing | Node.js 24; nothing to install; no network |

It never changes your project and it does not score it: the rubric does that, with your peer and your
instructor. A READY verdict means the evidence is there to be scored.
